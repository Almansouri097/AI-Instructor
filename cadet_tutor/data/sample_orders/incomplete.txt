ORDERS FOR BRIDGE ATTACK

Situation: enemy are at the bridge at GR 3312 5540, about 8 of them with a machine gun, preparing to blow it.

Mission: 2nd Platoon seizes the bridge at GR 3312 5540 by 0630 in order to stop it being destroyed.

Execution: 1 Section fire support from the hedge line, 2 Section assaults across the bridge, 3 Section follows up and holds the east bank. H-hour 0600.
