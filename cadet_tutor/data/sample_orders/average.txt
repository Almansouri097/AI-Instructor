OPERATION ORDER - EXERCISE GRANITE FORD

1. SITUATION
Enemy: some enemy soldiers are at the bridge, maybe a section. They might blow it up.
Friendly: rest of the company is attacking too.
Weather is rainy in the morning.

2. MISSION
2nd Platoon attacks the bridge tomorrow morning.

3. EXECUTION
1 Section gives fire support from the hedge. 2 Section attacks over the bridge. 3 Section follows and holds the other side. We start at 0600 from the track.

4. SERVICE SUPPORT
Bring ammo and water. Casualties go back to the hedge.

5. COMMAND AND SIGNAL
I will be with 2 Section. Radio on the platoon net.
